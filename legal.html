Risk & privacy

Read this before you trade.

Risk

  • $KESTREL is a memecoin. It has no intrinsic value and there is no expectation of financial return.
  • Memecoins are extremely volatile. Most lose nearly all of their value, often within hours. Only use money you can afford to lose entirely.
  • Kestrel is a screening and research tool. Its calls are probabilistic estimates with published test results, not advice or recommendations to buy or sell anything.
  • Practice results use live prices and realistic costs but no real money. Real fills can be worse, especially in thin pools.

Custody

  • Kestrel never holds, sees or asks for your private keys or seed phrase. If anything ever asks you for them, it isn't Kestrel.
  • Real trades are built by Jupiter's public API and must be approved in your own wallet. You can review every transaction there before it is sent.
  • The site has no backend. There is nothing to deposit into and no account to create.

Privacy

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels.
  • Your practice run and preferences are stored only in your own browser (localStorage keys starting with kestrel.). Clearing site data removes them.
  • To work, your browser talks directly to public services: GeckoTerminal, DexScreener, RugCheck and Jupiter for market data, quotes and balances, and PublicNode to check transaction status. Those services see your IP address and, for balances and trades, your public wallet address, under their own policies.
  • Kestrel is not affiliated with any of those services, with pump.fun, Axiom, or any wallet provider.
